📉 Compression — flexural buckling (E3)

Fcr from AISC 360 §E3; Pn = Fcr·Ag; φcPn = 0.9 Pn. Enter K, L, and r in consistent length units (in). E defaults to 29000 ksi if omitted. Torsional buckling not checked.

🧷 Tension rod / net area

Simplified: Pn = Fy·An; φtPn = 0.9 Pn. Supply net area An (in²) including thread deductions as appropriate.

📐 Bending — plastic moment (compact W-shape idealization)

Mn = Fy·Zx (kip·in) → Mn = Mn/12 (kip·ft). φbMn = 0.9 Mn. LTB and flange local buckling are not checked here.

✂️ Shear — web yielding (G2.1)

Vn = 0.6 Fy Aw; φvVn with φv = 1.0 for web yielding. Buckling and stiffeners are not modeled.
